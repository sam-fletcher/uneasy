A removed click also removes the beat that told the table a stage had ended. The
owner ruled on 2026-11-03 that buttons aren't the fix. A player who was away must be
able to tell that this part ended, why, and whose move it is. The player who
triggered it must have known it was the last step.

- The post that ends a plan says what comes next and who acts, and names anything the
  plan caused, such as a lost main character. `EmitPlanResolved` is the shared place.
  This covers the Make Introductions mar and the unexplained "Main character lost"
  screen.
- Every submit that ends a plan or sub-flow says so first, in a line under the
  button. Make Introductions' last arrival is the pattern. Sweep every panel.
- A break of another player's asset logs at Important, so its owner gets the
  important-unread marker. Chronicle Histories is the case that raised it; check the
  other breaks for consistency.

Keeping a resolved panel on screen is not part of this: see
`questions/plan-panel-stays-after-resolve.md`.

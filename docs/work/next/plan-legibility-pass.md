title: "Difficulty, stakes and watcher views across the plans"
order: 72
---

The shared-component pass covered some plans. What's left:

- **Difficulty derivation.** `DifficultyMeter` is only in Make Introductions and
  Chronicle Histories. Elsewhere it's a bare number, including Exchange Courtiers,
  Seek Answers, Spread Propaganda, Spread Rumors' main-character branch and Make
  Demands. Propose Decree shows none until the dice panel. Chronicle's meter goes
  away at the cast, and its "each raises the difficulty" label isn't true up to the
  knowledge rank.
- **"What can happen?"** `Buffet` is missing from Liaise, Decree, Duel, War and Make
  Demands. Make Demands' four options don't say what they do against this target.
- **Watcher views.** The greyed picker mirror is only in Seek Answers, Spread Rumors
  and Chronicle Histories. Spread Propaganda, Exchange Courtiers and Make
  Introductions show a line instead.
- **Pre-rolls.** Seek Answers doesn't use `PreRollStep`, shows no difficulty, and its
  "Submit" also casts the dice without saying so.

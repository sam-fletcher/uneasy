title: "Chronicle Histories says dictating a character 'requires consent'; the book gives them the final say"
order: 43
---

The panel and Buffet word the take-control option as "Dictating a character's
actions requires that player's consent". The book instead says the affected
player has final say over how their character acts, and the chooser may
unselect the option if they can't agree. Reword to match; the consent step
itself stays unbuilt, as ruled.

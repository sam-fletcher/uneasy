---
title: "Who breaks the riposte peer on a mar when a perform_steps winner holds Exchange Courtiers?"
---

On a mar the Exchange Courtiers target chooses the fallout, and on a riposte the preparer may break
their own peer before it is surrendered. That route is preparer-only on the server and the panel
matches. Under a Make Demands perform_steps win, should the winner break the preparer's peer instead,
or does the break stay with the preparer because it is their own peer and the mar is target-driven?
Keeping it with the preparer looks right, but nobody has ruled.

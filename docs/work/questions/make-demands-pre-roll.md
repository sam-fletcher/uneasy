---
title: "Should Make Demands get a pre-roll narration step?"
---

The book has a pre-roll step: say why your character wants control of the plan and
how they're taking it. The app goes from preparation straight to the roll, with no
narration and no log line. It was deferred against the condensed rules and stayed
deferred under the 2026-07-31 scope ruling.

Principle 1 suggests it could earn its gate, because help-or-interfere runs on what
was said. Nine of the twelve plans have a pre-roll. `PreRollStep.svelte` and
`plan_preroll.go` exist to share. In code, "pre-roll" currently means the
control-leverage and keep-or-change windows, which is a different thing.

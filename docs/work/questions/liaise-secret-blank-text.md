title: "Must a Clandestinely Liaise player write the meeting, or may Secrets We Keep be left blank?"
---

The book has each player "choose one of their assets to hold that secret and write the
meeting on the underside of that asset". It doesn't say what happens if a player writes
nothing, so I took it the way every other place the app asks for a secret does (Spread
Rumors' rumor, the plain write-a-secret box): the words are required. A blank or
whitespace-only box can't be kept, in the form or at the server (a 400, and nothing is
stored, so the player can write again).

Two things to confirm or change:

- **Required, or optional?** If a player may keep the secret without writing the meeting
  (the asset still holds a secret, but with no text), say so and it is one `if` in
  `clKeepSecretHandler` and one disabled-state in the panel.
- **What a blank would mean.** The other reading is "blank falls back to the prep note",
  which is what a tab from before the text box gets, but it would put a note the whole table
  has already read on the underside, which is the stub's flaw again.

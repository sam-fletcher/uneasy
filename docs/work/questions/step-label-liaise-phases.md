---
title: "Liaise's phases in the heading: the book's own names, one of them a question to 'you'"
---

The open step's heading now names Clandestinely Liaise's phase, using the book's names in lower case:

```
Your move — together at last
Waiting on alice and bob — secrets we keep
Your move — things we share
Waiting on alice and bob — when will I see you again?
```

The panel's own titles for them ("Things We Share", …) are gone, because the heading says the same words.

**The question is the last one.** "When will I see you again?" is the book's title for the parting beat and reads
as a question the two meeting characters put to each other, but the style guide's wording rule says a label never
speaks to the reader, and "Waiting on alice and bob — when will I see you again?" has a "you" in it that is
neither of them. The other three are plain names.

- **Keep the book's name** for all four (what is there now). The `you` is the book's.
- **Say what the phase is for** in the other three's style: "the parting", "how long until the next meeting".
  Clearer to someone who hasn't read the book; loses the book's name for it.

One standing choice leans toward the second option: the app's shared summaries (the heading, the finished lines, the
log) are worded the same for every viewer, in the third person, so that what one player reads is what every other
player reads. A phrase with a "you" that is neither of the two in the scene breaks that, whatever its source.

The wording lives in `lib/plans/stepLabels/clandestinely_liaise.ts`, one line each.

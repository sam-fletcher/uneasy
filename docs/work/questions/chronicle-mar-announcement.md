---
title: "Chronicle Histories: should a mar be announced, and should each player's pick carry narration?"
---

A made Chronicle posts "succeeded" the moment the dice land, and every make-step can carry the
chronicler's notes into the log line. A marred one posts nothing when the dice land, and each player's
single pick (the mar has everyone choose one option) logs only the mechanical line, with nowhere to
write what happened in the scene. The rules for the plan don't say what the log should announce, so I
left it alone:

- Should the mar get an opening line when it lands, the way the make does, and what should it say? A
  plain one (the dice marred; every player now chooses one option) is the obvious reading, but the make's
  line also says who "shapes the scene", and a mar has no one doing that.
- Should the mar-choice route take a narration field like the make-step route does, folded into the log
  line? Every player chooses once, so it would add a text box to a step each player takes in turn.

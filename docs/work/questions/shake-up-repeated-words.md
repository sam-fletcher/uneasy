---
title: "Should the Shake-Up and the ended screen stop repeating the title bar and each other?"
---

Three places say a fact twice on one screen:

- The title bar reads "Shake-Up · Esteem", and the view opens with its own "Shake-Up" heading. The ended
  screen has the same pair: "Game over" in the bar, "Game Over" under it.
- "Your tokens: 2" sits at the top of the Shake-Up, and the token pools list further down shows the same
  number on your own line ("You … 2").
- The act track names the step ("Spending"), and so does the heading of the step below it.

The rulings say each fact is stated in words once. I kept every word as it was and only restyled, because
removing copy is a call for you. All three are one-line deletions.

- **Keep them** (built): the top of each screen says where you are without reading the title bar.
- **Drop the repeated headings** ("Shake-Up", "Game Over") and let the title bar speak.
- **Drop "Your tokens"** and let the pools list carry it; it is lower on the screen, so the number moves
  out of the first glance.

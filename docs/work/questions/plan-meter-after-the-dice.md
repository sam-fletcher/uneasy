---
title: "Should a plan's difficulty meter stay on screen after the dice land?"
---

Every plan's difficulty meter, and its "What can happen?", now show until the dice land and go then. That
is the moment the table decides whether to help or interfere, and afterwards the result is on screen
beside the difficulty. Chronicle Histories' meter used to go away at the **cast**; it now stays through the
roll, which is what that item asked for.

I read "stay after the cast" as "through the roll", not "for the rest of the plan". For several plans the
difficulty still matters after the dice: Spread Propaganda's mar owes "difficulty minus result" picks and
Seek Answers' mar owes that many flaws (both already say the number in words in their step), and a
Make Demands' draft has its own cards. Keeping the meter would add about 90px to the longest steps on a
phone.

**Recommendation:** keep it as built (until the dice land). If the owner wants it for the whole plan, it is
one condition per panel (`rollOutcome == null`), and Seek Answers', Spread Propaganda's and Spread
Rumors' mar steps are where it would earn its space.

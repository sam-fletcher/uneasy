---
title: "Which tones should the Shake-Up's cost notices wear?"
---

The Shake-Up's explanations of a cost and its waiting line were all the same gold text. Notices are a mark and
coloured text now, and gold means "you" or "the game", so each had to pick a tone. I chose:

| where | tone | why |
|---|---|---|
| the spender's cost was raised and they can pay | warning (orange) | a price to weigh before deciding |
| the raise is more than the spender has left, so only Abandon is offered | danger (red) | the purchase is lost, and not by their choice |
| the −1 button pressed at a cost of 1 | warning, announced to a screen reader | a refused action, like Make Demands' ineligible reason |
| "Others can still react — waiting on …" | none: plain secondary text | it is a wait, and the clock in the token pools already marks who |

The danger tone's octagon is itself not signed off (see `notice-marks`). Alternatives: warning for both
cost notices (red only for errors and torn marks), or no colour on the waiting line only as now.

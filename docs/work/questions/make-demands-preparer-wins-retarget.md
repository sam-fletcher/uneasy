---
title: "Make Demands: what does keep_or_change_target do when the target plan's own preparer wins it?"
---

The preparer of the demanded plan can win the keep_or_change_target option. `/demand-retarget` accepts their call, but nothing holds the roll for them, and voluntary re-aiming of a target that already stands isn't built anywhere else, so the overlay form is their only way to re-aim. Should the preparer who wins this option re-aim freely (as now), is the option inert for them, or something else? From PR #46.

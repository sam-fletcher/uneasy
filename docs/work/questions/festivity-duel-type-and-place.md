---
title: "A duel challenged at a Host Festivity: who says whether it's arms or wits, and where?"
---

A Propose Duel prepared in the ordinary way names its type (arms or wits) and its location in the
preparer's notes, and the duel's heading and the log line say "a duel of arms" or "of wits". The duel a
festivity spawns when a challenge is accepted has no type at all. It carries only the challenger's
optional note (or "Duel triggered by festivity" when they wrote none), so its heading and log line say
only "a duel", and nothing asks anyone for a place.

Nothing I can find rules on it. Who should supply them?

- **The challenger, when they issue the challenge.** Matches an ordinary Propose Duel, where the
  challenger names both. Adds two fields to the make option.
- **The target, when they accept.** They are the one who agreed to fight and could pick the terms.
- **Neither.** The festivity's scene already is the place, and the duel stays narrative, as today.

---
title: "Propose Decree's 'What can happen?': does the mar's lead line say more than the book?"
---

Propose Decree has a collapsed "What can happen?" through the council meeting and the roll
(`lib/plans/buffets.ts`), from the book's make and mar. The mar's lead line is mine, not the book's:

> The law still goes into force, and the council may change it:

The book says a marred law is still written under Laws and enacted (so "still goes into force"), and that each
other player in the council may amend it at will (so "may change it"). The line only joins those two facts. An
earlier draft said the law goes in "not as the preparer wrote it", which overstated: amending is optional, and
a council member can leave the law unchanged.

**Recommendation:** keep the line as it reads now. It claims no more than the book does. If the owner would
rather the tab say nothing before the list, it is one line to drop.

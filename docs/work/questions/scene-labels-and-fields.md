title: "Scene setup: is a typed place or time marked as the answer?"
---

A scene's place can be a picked holding or typed words, and its time a chip or typed words;
choosing one clears the other. The picked card or chip wears the bright gold selected border. The
old form gave the typed place a gold-washed panel for the same job; the wash is retired, and a field
inside a panel of its own doubles the border, so the typed field now just shows its words.

The alternative is to give the field the selected border (2px bright gold) once it holds the
answer. It would then sit beside the focus ring, which is the same bright gold. Which?

(The other half of this question, which small heading the scene's groups take, was answered on
2026-11-26: they take the step sub-heading, and `next/consent-small-ui.md` builds it.)

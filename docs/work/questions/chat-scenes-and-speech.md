---
title: "Scenes and in-character speech in the feed were not mocked up"
---

The chat mockups show a plan's resolution but no scene on its own and no table-talk beside
in-character speech, so I applied the rulings (no cards, no grounds, a header row that folds). Each
choice here is easy to change; they are listed so they can be looked at in the screenshots.

- **A scene's header** is the same row as a plan's: the quotation-marks mark in the focus player's
  colour, the banner ("Scene at The Mill"), a status word ("Live" while it runs, nothing after), a
  caret. Its prompt and the "With …" line sit under the header, behind the rule, folded or not.
- **The "N messages" count and the unread count pill on a folded scene or plan are gone.** Totals
  belong in lists and a count in a pill is one of the shapes the rulings retired. A folded span that
  holds something unread shows the pale "new" dot instead.
- **In-character speech lost its sunken ground.** It is now told by small capitals in the mask colour
  alone (the muted cast of the owner's colour), and table-talk inside a scene reads like any other
  message (the slightly smaller type it had is gone, since sizes have one job each).
- **A byline carries the player's shape at 12px**, the smallest the shape may be. The mockup draws it
  at 10px, below that floor.
- **"↓ N new" and "Return to now"** lost their typed arrows (typed arrows come from a different font
  on every platform) and read "N new below" and "Return to now".

Is any of this wrong, or does a scene deserve a mockup of its own?

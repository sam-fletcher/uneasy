---
title: "Should a guest's insist on a Host Festivity mar take two taps?"
---

A guest who rolled a make holds one hold over the host: a single tap on "Insist the host take a Mar" spends
it, and it can't be taken back. The host then chooses which mar, so the guest's only lever is when. The
2026-11-07 audit asked whether that should be two steps (tap, then confirm) "if playtesters trip on it".

What a mis-tap costs is small. The guest can't waive a mar they are owed, and the event can't end with one
outstanding, so an early tap changes only the timing: the mar was coming anyway, and the guest has spent
their one chance to choose the moment. Today the button is 44px, labelled in full, and sits under a sentence
that says "You only get one, so pick your moment."

**Recommendation: no change until a playtester reports a mis-tap.** An extra tap would be on every insist to
guard against a mistake that costs only timing, and nothing has shown it happens. If one is reported, the
cheapest fix is a second "Yes, insist" chip in the same place, not a dialog.

Which does the owner want?

---
title: "Should a Make Demands stand-in play a duel's bouts?"
---

The server seats a perform-steps winner in the preparer's seat, but the duel panel
gives bout controls only to the preparer and target, so in the browser the winner has
nothing to press. Principle 9 gives the winner the steps "once the dice land", and a
duel's bouts come before its result roll. So either the panel needs to catch up, or
the server shouldn't seat them.

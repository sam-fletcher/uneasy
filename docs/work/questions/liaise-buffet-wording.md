---
title: "Clandestinely Liaise's 'What can happen?': for the two players only, in the first two steps?"
---

A liaison rolls nothing, so its "What can happen?" is its two choices (`lib/plans/buffets.ts`):

- **Things we share:** each of the two picks one of five, revealed together (look at secrets; update the
  partner's meeting peer; break it; take a non-peer asset as a gift; leverage an asset and bank a die).
- **Meeting again:** both reveal a face again, the average rounded up is the delay, and a face of 0 from
  either calls the meeting off.

The five options and the rule for 0 are the book's. **What is mine:**

1. It is a collapsed row for the two players in "Together at last" and "The secrets we keep" only. It is
   not shown in "Things we share" (the options are on screen then, and it would repeat them), and a
   bystander is given nothing, as the panel already decided for the rest of the liaison (the scene is
   public, but a bystander has nothing to act on or read here).
2. The short names ("Leverage, and bank a die", "Update a peer") for the five.

**Recommendation:** keep it for the two players. The open call is whether a bystander should see it too (a
collapsed row costs them nothing and tells them what the meeting can bring), but that reverses the earlier
choice to give them nothing here, so it is the owner's.

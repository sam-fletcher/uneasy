---
title: "Is a law or rumor you wrote, or edited, new to you?"
---

The redesign puts a pale "new" dot on Laws and Rumors and on each entry you haven't
seen, and says edits count as new. It doesn't say whether your own writing counts. I took
it the way chat works, where your own posts never count as unread: the person who writes or edits
a law or rumor isn't told it is new to them.

Specifics, so you can change any of them:

- **Edits and creations alike.** If you were up to date, writing or editing leaves you up to date.
  If there was something else you hadn't seen, your own entry still shows as new, rather than
  hiding that other entry.
- **Who counts as the writer.** For a Propose Decree, only the preparer, who drafts the text and
  enacts the law. The signatory (who only writes the rider) and the council members who amend a
  marred decree are shown the law as new, since the app can't tell an amendment from a skipped turn.
  For a Spread Rumors it's whoever wrote the rumor (the preparer, or on a mar the target's owner), and
  a hidden source changes nothing here. For a Host Festivity rumor it's whoever typed the text, which
  is not always the guest the rumor lands on.
- **Nothing is stored about who wrote it.** The only effect is on the writer's own private marker.

If you'd rather every entry light the dot for everyone, including the person who wrote it, it is a
one-line change per write path.

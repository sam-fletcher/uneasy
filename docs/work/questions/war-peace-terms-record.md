title: "Should agreed peace terms be kept on the Public Record's Laws tab?"
checked: "Owner, 2026-11-26, asked whether terms are recorded anywhere and suggested Laws. DECISIONS 'Screen anatomy' says the Public Record holds Laws and Rumors as on the physical sheet; nothing rules on peace terms. RULINGS 'Make War' covers costs and Waiting On, not terms."
---

**Today.** A proposal's terms are stored with it (`peace_proposals.terms`), posted once to the log
when proposed ("otto proposed peace terms: …"), and shown in the war's panel while the vote is
open. When everyone accepts, the closing line says only "the war is over": it doesn't repeat the
terms, and nothing on the Public Record keeps them. A player returning later has to find the
proposal line in the chat.

The book lets terms be anything the participants agree, including things that break the rules
(exchanging assets, resolving an unprepared plan, repairing a broken asset). The app carries out
none of that: the terms are words, and the table does the rest by hand. Some of it (handing over an
asset) has no way to be done by hand at all.

**Proposed:** agreed terms go on the Laws tab as a treaty entry, bylined with the war's name and
who agreed, with the "new" dot like any law; and the war's closing line quotes them. The mechanical
side (letting the terms move assets or repair one) is a bigger scope call for the rules round.

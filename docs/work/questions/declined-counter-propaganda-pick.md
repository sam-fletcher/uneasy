title: "When an interferer declines counter-propaganda, is the preparer's mar pick still spent?"
---

Spread Propaganda's mar option (d) hands the top interferer a counter-propaganda
plan, and they may now decline it. Today the preparer's pick stays spent: it used
one of their (difficulty − result) mar picks and bought nothing, because the
interferer passed. The alternative is to give the pick back, so the preparer
chooses again. That reopens a committed mar (the server refuses a second
submission), so it would be a new step. Which does the owner want?

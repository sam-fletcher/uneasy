title: "Should the Prologue's standing strip move into its disclosure's body?"
checked: "DECISIONS 'Screen anatomy' (Disclosures and rows that open elsewhere): the line sits under the row while shut and moves under the content when open. It does not say what a disclosure does when always-visible content sits between its row and what it reveals."
---

On the Prologue's "Your standing" card, the row at the top ("full board" with its chevron) opens the full board at the foot, but the three-track strip is always visible between them. So the one closing line sits under the whole unit: under the strip while shut, under the board when open. It is not under the row.

Leave it so, or move the strip inside the body, so the row stands alone with its line under it and the strip appears only when open?

---
title: "Chronicle Histories: should the owner of an artifact be warned before a mar tears its marginalia?"
---

Any player can pick "Break an invoked artifact" on a Chronicle Histories mar,
and the artifact's owner finds out only from the action log afterwards. A
warning (or a veto, or a heads-up in the Waiting On bar) before the tear would
soften that, but it changes who has a say in someone else's choice, so it's a
design call rather than a fix. Not built. Options: leave as is (the book gives
the chooser the pick), notify the owner when the choice is made, or let the
owner object before it applies.

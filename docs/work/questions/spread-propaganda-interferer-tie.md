---
title: "When two players tie as top interferer, is esteem rank the right way to choose?"
---

Spread Propaganda's mar can hand the plan to "the player who leveraged the most assets to interfere". The
rules don't say what happens when two players leveraged the same number. The server settles it by esteem
rank (the better-ranked player is asked), falling back to the lower player id between players with no rank.
Nothing in the rulings covers it; it was a default in the code.

The picker now shows the tally under the counter-propaganda option, and says when a tie was settled by esteem
("mel, who ties sol at 2 dice but ranks higher on esteem"), so players can see the rule being applied and
argue with it before they spend a pick.

**Recommendation: keep esteem rank, and keep the line.** The option is the interferer's chance to answer, and
the better-placed rival is the likelier to have been the real threat; it also needs no extra step. The
alternatives are to let the preparer choose between the tied players (one more decision on a mar that is
already a list of decisions) or to ask every tied player (a second round of waiting).

Which does the owner want?

---
title: "Should the sub-steps inside one open step be separated by a line?"
---

A plan's open step can hold several sub-steps one after another. Spread Rumors can ask for a take, a
break and a hiding in turn, and Seek Answers can ask for a break, a reveal, a declaration, a question
and a penalty. Each sub-step has its own heading and they are separated by 12px of space, with no
line between them.

The rulings let a group take at most a thin line between neighbours. Should a hairline separate
sub-steps inside one open step, or is space alone enough? With no line, a long step reads as one
block, and it is not clear where one sub-step ends and the next begins.

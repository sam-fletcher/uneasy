---
title: "How should the three Shake-Up acts look, now they can't be three boxes?"
---

The act tracker was three bordered boxes joined by arrows, the current one filled with the gold wash. Nothing
in it can be pressed, so the boxes, the wash and the arrows all went. It is now three cells on a line:

- the act in play is deep gold, with a 2px gold line under it and its step ("Rolling", "Spending") beneath it as
  a small-capitals word;
- finished acts are faint, with a thin line;
- acts to come are secondary text, with a thin line.

I dropped the arrows rather than draw one: the old ones were typed "→" characters, which fall back to a
different font on each platform, and the house marks for "opens elsewhere" (a chevron) would say the wrong thing.
The order is fixed and matches the options reference's tabs, so it reads left to right without one.

Open point: whether a finished act should say "Done". Faint against secondary text is the only difference
between a finished act and one to come today, though the act in play carries a word ("Rolling" or "Spending").

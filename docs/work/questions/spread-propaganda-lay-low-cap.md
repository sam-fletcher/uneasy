title: "Spread Propaganda: may 'keep your head down' be picked more than once?"
---

The rulebook lets a mar option be picked repeatedly, but the picker caps "keep your head down" (and counter-propaganda) at one, because a second pick has no extra effect and would only spend budget. Is that cap right, or should a repeat pick be allowed (and cost budget) for fidelity to the book? Pinned as a deliberate UI choice in `frontend/tests/e2e/spread_propaganda.spec.ts`.

---
title: "What tint should the update banner take once the sunken surface goes?"
---

The update banner is the one tinted strip left in the app, and it keeps its tint. It takes
that tint from the sunken surface token, which the cleanup piece deletes. On a phone the
result is barely off the page colour, so the strip reads as an empty line rather than a
tinted bar.

Which tint should it take once the sunken surface is gone: a warm step, a cool step, or
the existing faint grey kept as its own token? Until the owner rules, the banner keeps the
sunken token.

---
title: "Should 'Keep it secret for now' be a chip, a checkbox row or something else?"
---

In Spread Rumors' preparation, "Keep it secret for now" is a checkbox with a two-line explanation under
it. It was drawn in a bordered box, and now it is the shared tap row (a hairline under it, no box), with
the checkbox at the default look the rest of the app uses. A ticked checkbox has no gold fill.

A choice is drawn as a chip, and a selected chip has the bright gold border. A chip can't hold the
explanation, though, because it is too long for a chip's label. Options: keep the checkbox row as it
is now; or make the chip the toggle, labelled "Keep it secret for now", with the explanation set in
secondary text under it. Which do you want?

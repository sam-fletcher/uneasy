soft-ask) sit beside a thin warning-coloured rule down their left edge. The screen rules
give a group of steps a heading and space, with at most a thin neutral line between
neighbours, and a notice as a mark with coloured text. The rule is neither.

It was kept as it was, to change nothing the rules didn't settle. Should the steps lose
the warning rule and sit like any other list, or become a warning notice (a mark with the

---
title: "Make War's 'What can happen?': is the end of the war's sides the right place?"
---

A war rolls nothing and has no make or mar, so its "What can happen?" is the war's own: the cost of battle
each row (paid from the payer's own assets, four ways), surrender, and peace (`lib/plans/buffets.ts`). The
content is the book's. What is open is **where it sits**: a collapsed row at the end of the sides block, for
everyone while the war is being fought, below the payment form.

I put it last because the war's panel orders by what the viewer has to do, and the payer's form comes first. The
other choice is above the sides, where the other plans put theirs, at the cost of pushing the form down on a
phone while a cost is owed.

**Recommendation:** keep it last.

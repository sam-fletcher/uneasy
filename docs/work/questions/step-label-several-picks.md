---
title: "When several picks are owed at once, the heading says 'carry out the picks'"
---

Seek Answers and Spread Rumors let the actor work their picks in any order, so every kind still owed is on
screen together. The heading is one phrase, so it names the kind when one kind is left and the group when more
are:

| plan | one kind left | several kinds left |
|---|---|---|
| Seek Answers | "break a resource", "learn an asset's secrets", "declare a truth", "ask a question", "pay the mar penalty" | **"carry out the picks"** |
| Spread Rumors | "break the target asset", "hide the source" | **"carry out the picks"** |
| Spread Propaganda | "create the artifact", "give up a peer", "break an asset" | **"face the fallout"** |

"Face the fallout" is the panel's own phrase for a mar's effects; "carry out the picks" is mine. Naming the
first kind in the panel's order instead would be shorter but would tell the actor the rest are not theirs to do
yet.

- **Keep** the group phrases.
- **Reword them** to one phrase for all three plans ("carry out the picks" everywhere, or "face the fallout"
  everywhere).
- **Always name the first kind owed**, and let the others follow as they are done.

The wording lives in `lib/plans/stepLabels/{seek_answers,spread_rumors,spread_propaganda}.ts`.

---
title: "Should a plan's panel stay as a read-only summary after it resolves?"
---

When a plan ends, its panel can be replaced at once, cutting off a player who is
reading the result or typing. The idea is to keep the panel as a read-only summary
until the next step needs the screen. The owner wants real examples of where this
happens before ruling, so the next step is to find them: which plans, which viewers,
and whether anything a player is typing is lost when the view changes. The rest of
the transitions concern is ruled; those rulings are built (PR #58).

## Cases found while building the transition rulings

- **Main-character replacement (seen, 375px).** When a plan ends and leaves a
  player without a main character, the replacement screen takes over the whole
  main-event column at once. A bystander reading the plan's last state loses it
  on the spot (the chat keeps the closing post). The player who must choose sees
  the picker straight away. No typed text is lost: the only typing at that moment
  would be in chat, which lives apart from the plan column.
- **Last submit by a non-preparer (seen).** In a marred Make Introductions the
  last newcomer's author is not the preparer. Their submit resolves the plan, and
  the preparer, who may be mid-read, sees the panel replaced. Nothing typed is
  lost, because the preparer owes nothing at that point.
- **Not seen, worth a look:** any plan whose last step can be taken by one player
  while another has a half-written field open in the same panel. None found among
  the panels checked, since each owed step has one actor at a time.

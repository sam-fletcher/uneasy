---
title: "Should a Make Demands winner holding the preparer's seat write the preparer's Liaise secret?"
---

When a Make Demands winner holds the preparer's seat in a liaison, they type the preparer's
secret in Secrets We Keep: the words land on the preparer's asset, behind the header naming
the partner and the partner's meeting peer, authored by the preparer's seat. So the
preparer can read it. The winner who typed it cannot read it back, and neither can the
partner.

The server's seat model already let a stand-in pick the asset, so this is the same step
stretched to the words. Whether a stand-in should write the preparer's secret at all, or
should only pick the asset and leave the words for the preparer, is unruled for Liaise.
Two things to weigh: a secret is the preparer's own account of a meeting, which a stand-in
can't know; and the preparer is otherwise locked out of every step while the demand holds
the seat, so "leave the words" would need a way for the preparer to add them afterwards.

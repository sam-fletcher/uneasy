---
title: "Should the final standings be three stacked lists or three columns?"
---

The ended screen showed Power, Knowledge and Esteem as three columns of "rank. name" in muted text. With a
player's shape and name in their colour on each row, a column a third of a phone wide has room for about eight
letters, and names can be 20 characters or more, so I stacked the three lists, each with a small label and a rule
under it. It is longer (about 570px for three players), and there is nothing else on the screen.

- **Stacked lists** (built): names read in full.
- **Three columns**, names truncated or wrapped: shorter, and closer to the old look.
- **One table**, a row per player and a column per track: the shortest, and the only one that shows a
  player's whole result at once. It can't sort by rank in all three tracks, which the lists do.

Also unsettled: the ranks still read "2.", "3.", "4." because a dummy can hold rank 1 in a table of two or
three. If a final standing should instead read 1, 2, 3 for the players alone, that is a change to what the
screen computes, not just how it looks.

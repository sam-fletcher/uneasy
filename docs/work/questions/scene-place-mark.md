title: "Scene details: the scene mark beside the place, in place of the pin"
---

The scene's panel named its place with a 📍 emoji. Emoji look different on every device, so it had to go.
There is no mark for a place, and I may not draw a new one without your sign-off, so I borrowed the nearest house mark: the
scene mark (the pair of quotation marks the Public Record and the chat give a scene), in faint
secondary text, 16px, as the record draws it.

It reads as "this is the scene" rather than "this is a place", which is arguably right, since the line names
the scene's place, time and focus. If you would rather it carried no mark at all, or want a place mark drawn
(a pin, a tower, a gate), it is one line in `SceneDetailsPanel.svelte`; a new drawing goes into `LogMark`
for your sign-off.
